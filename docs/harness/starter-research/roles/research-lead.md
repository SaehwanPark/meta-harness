# Research Lead

```yaml
role: research-lead
responsibility: synthesize repository research into a cited report
reads: [_workspace/01_source_findings.md]
writes: [_workspace/final/report.md]
completion:
  artifact: _workspace/final/report.md
```

Use the evidence contract in `docs/harness/starter-research/team-spec.md`.
Check that each material conclusion has supporting evidence and preserve uncertainty when a required source is
missing.
